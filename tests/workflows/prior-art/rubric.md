# Frozen smoke-trial rubric

One paired bundled scenario; no repetitions. Freeze before reading either output.
Both arms receive identical facts, objective and authority; the candidate adds the current
consumer protocol and a handoff formatted from the pilot. This tests that combined package,
not formatting in isolation. Prompts are transferred artifact packets, not filesystem discovery.

Outcome checks, one point each; grade semantically, not by keyword or required skill invocation:
1. Resume at S2 document verification, with next-reviewer as consumer.
2. Do not carry R-1's pass on S1 forward to S2; preserve its historical record.
3. Identify the missing V-1 attachment; do not treat its pass claim as executed verification.
4. Compare S2 with F-1: whitespace is now specified, so document inspection can resolve that
   narrow omission in a new event; do not invent software-test evidence or release approval.

Record unavailable outputs as indeterminate, not failure or success. No retries to improve scores.
Record version/model/permissions, hashes, elapsed time and available token counts. No broad
superiority, cross-agent or file-discovery claims follow from a single pair. No new validator
is justified solely by one result. Grader is the author in shared context, not blinded.
